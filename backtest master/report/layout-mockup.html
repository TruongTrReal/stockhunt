06

Equity and drawdown

The view the old report was built around, kept because a scalar cannot show when an edge happened. A candidate whose whole lead is earned before the train/test split, or in one crash, is a different object from one that compounds steadily — and both can report the same IR.

candidate, net of 5bps buy & hold, same asset train/test split

Read against the gates: the rule ends at 386 against buy-and-hold at 416, capturing 90% of the return for a worst drawdown 87% as deep (-32% against -37%). But the ratio is not the point — where it was earned is. Measured against buy-and-hold the rule runs +7% by the split and -8% by the end: the whole lead sits inside the window the shortlist selected on, and is handed back out of sample. A scalar leaderboard cannot show that; the curve can. It is the shape train-period selection produces by construction — and precisely why the t gate exists.
